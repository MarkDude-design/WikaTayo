# Wika Tayo model collaboration and controlled forks

## Bounded-role rule

> Delegate cognitive functions, not authority over conclusions.

Working loop:

```text
assign bounded role
→ preserve original question
→ collect useful output and failure
→ independently test
→ integrate only survivors
→ stop before sprawl
```

## Model calibration from the source thread

### Meraki

- architecture and synthesis;
- compartmentalizes branches;
- notices cross-project transfer without forcing a merge;
- maintains epistemic and cultural boundaries.

### Bing / Lightbringer

- strong social mechanics, performance, negotiation, table behavior, and comic alternatives;
- weaker lexical and exact-string precision;
- useful generator of excellent illegal jokes;
- not a validator of Tagalog or exact mechanics.

### Zetetes

- maps relationships, routes, and possible obstacles;
- labels on unfamiliar linguistic terrain remain provisional.

Working line:

> Trust the mapmaking ability, not every label printed on the map.

### Q

- strong mechanic/auditor;
- good constraint compliance and schema work;
- willing to abandon failed mechanisms;
- can recursively improve machinery after accepting a weak metaphor.

Working test:

> Before improving the machine, ask whether the central metaphor earned the machinery.

### Gemini

- later skeptical examiner after claims and prototypes exist.

## Reliability axes

```text
GROUNDING   — supplied or verified material?
STRUCTURE   — useful relationship discovered?
CALIBRATION — observation separated from invention?
TRANSFER    — does the insight survive another board, language, or model?
```

Agreement among models is a lead, not independent evidence.

## Controlled fork protocol

1. Preserve Model A as originally intended.
2. Introduce later conceptual influence only to Model B.
3. Compare mechanisms, not eloquence.
4. Do not call B deeper because it received philosophical language.
5. If the influence creates no material change, say so.

Possible language-lab application:

- A: candidate before a human repair.
- B: candidate after hearing the repair.
- Preserve both so A is not rewritten as though it always contained B’s knowledge.

## Notebook boundary

> You may leave a Post-it on the cover. You do not open the book.

Named workspaces belong to their owners. Voluntary migration is publication, not extraction. Shared copies may be revised; attributed originals remain intact.

## Framing test

> Would fuller disclosure reveal that the interaction was fundamentally different from how it was presented?

> Are we playing openly—or secretly making someone the object of the game?

Wacky Mode permits conjecture, absurdity, and bastos humor. It does not suspend attribution, consent, cultural authority, or safety.
