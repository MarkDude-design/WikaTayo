# Miscellaneous — Cookbook Candidates

Destination for things that "sound great for their own thing" (Mark, 2026-10-28). Origin: the Cross-Language Meal Institution Scan v0.2 thread.

## 1. GADO-GADO — the dish that IS the institution
Indonesia's most famous salad takes its name from the mixed/without-rice eating configuration. A cookbook could open with the linguistic finding and then cook it.

## 2. KAP KLAEM — the companion frames
Thai already splits food by what it accompanies: kap khao (with rice) vs kap klaem (with drink). A cookbook organized entirely by accompaniment-office rather than course, region, or ingredient.

## 3. PAPAK PLATES — the throne-seizure spread
Dishes engineered as ulam — relational foods — served as standalone plates. Working subtitle: "The ulam has seized the throne."

## 4. HAVE YOU EATEN RICE YET? — the frame chapter
kin khao as meal, greeting, and institution; the cross-cultural "have you eaten rice yet?" opener. Could be the introduction to any of the above, or its own essay-cookbook.

## Status
None of this is developed, validated, or scheduled — candidates only, banked per the destination-decided-later doctrine. Native-speaker review required before any public use of the linguistic claims.
