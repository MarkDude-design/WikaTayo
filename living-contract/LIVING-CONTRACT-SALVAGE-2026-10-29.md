# Living Contract salvage

## Current position

The Living Contract is related to Wika Tayo but is not identical to the language game or the talk. Wika Tayo supplies language research; legal, artistic, relational, and display layers require their own review.

## Preserved ideas

- Generic and relationship-specific contract versions.
- Deep-Tagalog language with at least three native-speaker sign-offs before publication.
- Baybayin as a possible script of commerce and relationship.
- A 1:1 legal-language key rather than implying decorative script alone creates legal effect.
- Bamboo or other physical artifact as a later display form.
- Consulate or Library of Congress-level display ambition.
- Participant repairs as possible acknowledged amendments.
- Separate visible layers for legal text, relational promise, artistic expression, and reader-supplied meaning.

## First-use-case relationship

PJ Quesada / Ramar may become a first use case, especially around food and cultural work, but the framework must generalize beyond one person, restaurant, or event.

## Roles and gates

- Mark: relationships, logistics, scouting, human review, final permission.
- Native speakers: naturalness and meaning.
- Baybayin practitioners, including Kristian Kabuay if he accepts: script and cultural review.
- Legal counsel: enforceability and representation.
- Models: drafting alternatives, provenance organization, ambiguity analysis, comparison—not authority.

## Tensions to preserve

- ceremony versus enforceability;
- cultural recognition versus aesthetic borrowing;
- personal artifact versus reusable framework;
- stable legal identity versus living repair;
- invocation versus community-guided authorship;
- public display versus private relationship.

## Do not promote without review

- Historical Baybayin contract conventions.
- Legal effect of Baybayin presentation.
- Bahala/Bathala etymological or historical claims.
- Invocation language.
- Named relationships, quotations, donations, or commitments.
- Library of Congress or consulate involvement as confirmed.
