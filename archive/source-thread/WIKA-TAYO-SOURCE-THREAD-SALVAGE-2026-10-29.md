# Wika Tayo source-thread salvage

This packet preserves the non-talk project material from the open source conversation formerly used for Pistahan, Living Contract, Baybayin wordplay, poetry, human review, and model collaboration.

It is a navigation layer, not a replacement transcript. Exact quotations, intimate relational evidence, and unreviewed model output remain in their original or private homes.

## Project identity

Wika Tayo is the language home. It owns:

- words, phrases, definitions, and translations;
- ambiguity analysis;
- fragment-based expression;
- Tagalog and code-switching experiments;
- Baybayin-related language research;
- human recognition, repair, and naturalness evidence;
- poetic constraints and the future words/poetry workbench.

Consuming projects may use reviewed outputs without absorbing the language lab.

## Working principles

- Lower the admission standard for candidates; keep publication standards high.
- Distinguish sourced language, model proposal, player invention, and human-reviewed material.
- Recognition is not the same as naturalness.
- A repair may be evidence, affection, teaching, or all three.
- Models may map, generate, compare, and organize. Native speakers and relevant practitioners retain cultural and linguistic authority.
- Preserve provenance, uncertainty, failures, and alternate readings.
- Prefer small physical tests over elaborate untested rule systems.
- Do not flatten useful ambiguity merely to make a clean explanation.

## Active workstreams recovered

### Baybayin wordplay

Central creative proposition:

> Baybayin sometimes delegates work to the reader.

The project investigates how omitted final sounds, open syllables, remembered phrases, rhythm, and social completion can create play. This is a modern design hypothesis, not a settled claim about historical intent.

### Human review

The thread developed a lightweight, multiple-response review method:

```text
SINASABI KO   — I say this.
KILALA KO     — I recognize this.
NATUWA AKO    — This amused, moved, or interested me.
ANO BA ITO?   — What is this?
```

Possible continuation/completion category remains provisional:

```text
TULOY KO      — I continued or completed it automatically.
```

### Poetry / Makata

Functional review labels unexpectedly produced poetic structure. That discovery seeded a future poetry workbench based on fragments, attribution, omission, rhythm, and limited bridges rather than automatic polishing.

### #Words generator

The generator began as support for the word game but may become a larger project capable of storing phrases, sources, repairs, reactions, recognition channels, alternative orders, and private/public boundaries.

### Living Contract

The contract remains a related project with artistic, relational, legal, and cultural layers. It should not be reduced to the word game, and the word game should not be forced to carry the contract.

### Talk

Wika Tayo is also being developed as its own talk. Talk material is routed to the private review lane in `MarkDude-design/art-talk/wika-tayo-language/`; raw talk intake is not duplicated here.

## Teaching and recognition channels

Strong progressive seed:

```text
PEN PEN DE SARAPE_
PEN PEN DE _______
PEN PEN _________
```

Other recognition channels include:

- visual completion;
- spoken completion;
- rhythm and song memory;
- embodied/gestural memory;
- school and childhood memory;
- family and personal association;
- television/game-show patterns;
- delayed recollection.

## Light / UV layer

Threshold seed:

```text
AKI BITUI
→ AKI[NG] BITUI[N]
```

The reveal supplies one prepared reading. It does not invalidate a player’s reading or claim cultural authority.

`BAHALA NA` / `BA[T]HALA NA` is retained only as an artistic question. Roman-letter insertion and Baybayin glyph transformation are not mechanically identical; historical/etymological claims remain unverified.

## Publication and verification queue

- Exact provenance of Women of Malolos-derived candidates.
- Naturalness of deep-Tagalog candidates.
- Historical Baybayin omission claims.
- Bahala/Bathala relationship.
- Historical Baybayin contract practice.
- Legal effect of script presentation.
- Song, television, and game-expression rights.
- Practitioner review of every Baybayin glyph treatment.
- Consent and privacy review of relational human evidence.

## Preserved failures

- Model agreement treated as validation.
- Arbitrary internal-letter deletion treated as meaningful difficulty.
- A Q-generated blank-voting loop that remained an activity rather than a game.
- Overbuilt observer checklists.
- Treating every physical action as a printed tile.
- Treating the hidden UV answer as the winning answer.

These remain useful because they sharpen the design boundary.
